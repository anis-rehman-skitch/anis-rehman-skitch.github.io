About me

A power systems perspective.
A focus on the future.

38Research publications
32Journal articles
6Conference papers

I am a PhD researcher in Electrical Engineering at Griffith University, undertaking an Industry PhD project with RACE for 2030.

My doctoral work focuses on demand management through vehicle-to-grid and vehicle-to-home systems, bringing together microgrids, battery storage and electric vehicle charging. My broader interests include reinforcement learning, renewable energy integration and charging station optimisation.

My background combines electrical engineering research, industry experience and practical work in robotics and the Internet of Things. I hold a master's degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Azad Jammu and Kashmir and a bachelor's degree from Mirpur University of Science and Technology.
